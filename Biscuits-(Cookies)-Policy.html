Biscuits (Cookies) Policy

Effective Date: 01/01/2025

1. Introduction

At Galaxy Supply Zone, we take your privacy as seriously as a good cup of tea. That’s why we want to be upfront about the “biscuits” (also known as cookies) we use on our website. Sadly, we’re not talking about the chocolate digestives you dunk in your brew, but rather the small files that help our website remember who you are.

2. What Are Biscuits (Cookies)?

A biscuit (or “cookie” for our friends across the pond) is a small text file placed on your device when you visit our website. Unlike actual biscuits, these don’t crumble in your keyboard, but they do help us make your experience smoother and more enjoyable.

3. Types of Biscuits We Use

We use the following types of biscuits:

  • Essential Biscuits: Absolutely necessary for our website to function. Without these, our site would be as useful as a soggy custard cream.

  • Performance Biscuits: These let us see how you use our site, so we can improve things for you and future visitors. All info is anonymous—no nosing about in your biscuit tin!

  • Functionality Biscuits: Help us remember your preferences (like language and region), so you don’t have to keep repeating yourself.

  • Targeting/Advertising Biscuits: Occasionally, we use these to show you relevant ads or content. Don’t worry—we don’t share your tea or your info with just anyone.

4. Why We Use Biscuits

Biscuits help us:

  • Keep the website running properly

  • Remember your settings and preferences

  • Analyse how our website is used, so we can make it better (a bit like tweaking a recipe!)

  • Provide you with content and offers that may actually be useful, not just clutter

5. Managing Your Biscuits

You can manage, munch, or even delete our digital biscuits at any time via your browser settings. Just be aware, refusing biscuits might mean some parts of our website don’t work as intended—no one likes a broken biscuit.

Here’s how to adjust your settings on popular browsers:

6. Third-Party Biscuits

Some biscuits are delivered by third parties (such as Google Analytics) to help us understand how people use our website. These third-party biscuits follow their own privacy rules, but rest assured, we only partner with reputable “biscuit bakers.”

7. Consent

By using our website, you agree to us serving up a selection of biscuits, unless you’ve set your browser to reject them. You can withdraw consent at any time by adjusting your browser settings or using our handy cookie consent tool.

8. Changes to This Policy

We might update this Biscuits Policy now and then (fresh batch, anyone?). We’ll post any changes right here with a new date at the top.

9. Contact

Questions about our biscuits (or cookies)? Email us at info@galaxysz.com and we’ll put the kettle on.
